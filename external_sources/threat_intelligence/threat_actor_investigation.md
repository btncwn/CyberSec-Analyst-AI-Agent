# Threat Actor Investigation

## Investigation Process

1. Identify malware family.
2. Identify TTPs.
3. Identify ATT&CK techniques.
4. Identify infrastructure.
5. Identify victimology.

## Intelligence Sources

- MISP
- ATT&CK
- Vendor Reports
- Sandbox Analysis

## Analyst Output

- Threat Summary
- ATT&CK Mapping
- Detection Opportunities
- Recommended Controls
